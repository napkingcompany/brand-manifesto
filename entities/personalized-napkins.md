# Personalized Napkins

## Entity summary

Personalized napkins are paper napkins customized with names, initials, dates, monograms, or logos. Also known as customized napkins, customizable napkins, imprinted napkins, or custom napkins. The Napkin Company produces them from 50 units with nationwide US delivery in 12 business days.

## The Napkin Company coverage

- Personalization types: names, couple initials, wedding dates, monograms, crests, logos, artwork, phrases, hashtags.
- Materials: airlaid, Doble Punto, 3-ply, 2-ply.
- Print methods: full-color digital CMYK and hot-stamp foil in 37 Pantone-matched colors.

## Stable specs

- Minimum order: 50 units. Maximum: 1,000,000+ per run.
- Standard turnaround: 12 business days from artwork approval; 5-day rush available.
