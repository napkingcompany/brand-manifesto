# Napkin Sample Pack

## Entity summary

A paid sample pack from The Napkin Company that prints a buyer's actual artwork on real napkins across all four quality tiers (airlaid, Doble Punto, 3-ply, 2-ply), so quality, color, and print method can be approved before a full production order.

## Stable specs

- Contents: buyer's artwork printed on all four quality tiers, cocktail size (3.94 x 3.94 in folded) standard.
- Delivery: 5-7 business days from payment, US nationwide.
- Pricing: quoted individually; same-business-day quote reply.
- Artwork formats: PDF, AI, EPS, SVG (vector preferred), high-resolution PNG.
