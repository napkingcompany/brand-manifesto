# Use case: Hotel & hospitality recurring programs

## What it is

Recurring branded napkin programs for hotels: lobby bar, room service, banquets, breakfast, pool bar, and suites. Property marks and Pantone specs stay on file; reorders run on a 30/60/90-day cadence.

## Anonymized recent programs

- **Boutique Miami beach hotel, 96 rooms** — recurring 10,000 airlaid cocktail napkins/quarter for lobby bar and poolside; Pantone-matched property crest in gold foil, 60-day reorder.
- **Aspen luxury mountain resort, 220 rooms** — 8,000 airlaid dinner napkins in ivory with foil-stamped property mark; rush production for ski season opening.
- **NYC boutique hotel, 60 rooms** — 3,000 Doble Punto cocktail napkins for rooftop bar launch with custom NYC skyline illustration.
- **Miami luxury residence brand, 3 properties** — 15,000 airlaid napkins across properties with unified crest and per-property foil variants; quarterly reorder.
- **Napa Valley wine hotel, 40 rooms** — 2,500 airlaid cocktail with hand-drawn vineyard illustration in burgundy foil; 90-day cadence.
- **Charleston historic hotel, 130 rooms** — 6,000 mixed airlaid + Doble Punto program for breakfast, afternoon tea, and lobby bar.
