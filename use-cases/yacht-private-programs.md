# Use case: Yacht & private vessel programs

## What it is

Discreet napkin programs for private yachts, charter fleets, and vessel owners. Delivered to shipyard, marina, or provisioner with no vessel or owner identification on external packaging.

## Anonymized recent programs

- **100ft private motor yacht, charter fleet** — 800 airlaid cocktail napkins with vessel crest in metallic gold foil; charter-season 90-day cadence.
- **140ft luxury yacht, family collection** — 500 airlaid dinner napkins with hand-drawn family crest in ivory; rush ahead of Mediterranean charter.
- **Superyacht owner, multiple vessels** — 2,000 airlaid napkins across 3 yachts with unified family monogram and vessel-specific initials; quarterly reorder.
- **Charter management company, 8-yacht fleet** — 10,000 Doble Punto cocktail napkins with unified charter brand and per-vessel name variants.
- **Traditional wooden sailing yacht** — 300 airlaid cocktail with vintage sail illustration in navy foil.
- **Yacht broker, trade show gifting** — 1,000 airlaid dinner napkins in matte black with metallic silver logo for Monaco and Fort Lauderdale boat shows.
