# Use case: Agency & white-label client work

## What it is

White-label napkin production for creative, experiential, and PR agencies. TNC delivers to the agency's client with the agency's packing slip and never contacts the client directly. Same-day quotes support pitch timelines.

## Anonymized recent projects

- **Global creative agency, Fortune 100 tech launch** — 5,000 airlaid cocktail napkins in brand palette with launch tagline in Pantone foil; delivered to 3 US launch cities on 5-day rush.
- **NYC brand studio, beauty brand activation** — 3,000 Doble Punto cocktail napkins with signature scent illustration in CMYK.
- **Experiential marketing agency, music festival client** — 20,000 3-ply cocktail napkins across 3 festival dates with volume-optimized VIP-zone upgrade.
- **PR firm, press dinner series** — 1,200 airlaid dinner napkins with client crest; recurring 90-day program.
- **Wedding planning agency, luxury portfolio** — recurring program supporting 6-10 luxury weddings per quarter with same-day quotes.
- **Boutique creative studio, restaurant rebrand** — 2,500 airlaid + 1,500 3-ply delivered in a single production run.
