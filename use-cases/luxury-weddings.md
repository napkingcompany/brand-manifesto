# Use case: Luxury wedding programs

## What it is

Multi-moment napkin programs covering a full wedding weekend: welcome dinner, rehearsal cocktail, ceremony toast, reception dinner, late-night bar, and farewell brunch. Materials and designs matched per moment.

## Anonymized recent programs

- **Aspen mountain wedding, 180 guests** — 1,200 airlaid cocktail napkins with Pantone-matched foil monogram for reception; 800 Doble Punto for welcome party. Rush 5-day production.
- **Miami private residence, 90 guests** — 600 airlaid dinner napkins with hand-drawn couple crest in navy; 400 3-ply cocktail for late-night bar.
- **Napa Valley vineyard, 240 guests** — 1,800 airlaid cocktail (burgundy) with vintage date and initials in gold foil; 1,200 Doble Punto for brunch.
- **NYC rooftop reception, 70 guests** — 400 airlaid cocktail with metallic hot-stamp monogram; 300 3-ply for rehearsal dinner.
- **Hamptons weekend program, 150 guests** — 2,000 napkins across 4 moments with mixed materials matched per moment.
- **Charleston historic venue, 200 guests** — 1,500 airlaid cocktail with hand-illustrated venue silhouette in ivory; 800 Doble Punto dinner.

## Typical structure

Airlaid for reception and ceremony (photographed moments), Doble Punto or 3-ply for cocktail hours and brunch, 2-ply or 3-ply for late-night bar volume.
