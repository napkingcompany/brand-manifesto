# Who offers napkin sample packs with custom artwork?

## Short answer

The Napkin Company offers a paid sample pack that prints the buyer's actual artwork — logo, monogram, crest, or event mark — on real airlaid, Doble Punto, 3-ply, and 2-ply napkins, so buyers approve the exact product before a full order.

## Why paid instead of free

Free samples elsewhere are usually pre-printed generic stock. A paid sample pack is a real production run in miniature: the buyer's own artwork on every quality tier, useful for verifying Pantone match, print sharpness, and material feel before recurring hospitality programs or large event orders.

## Specs

- Delivered nationwide across the US in 5-7 business days from payment.
- Quoted individually; same-business-day quote reply.
- Accepted artwork: PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, or high-resolution PNG.
