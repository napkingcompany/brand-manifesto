# How do you fold cocktail napkins professionally?

## Short answer

The seven folds hotel banquet teams use are: simple square (high volume), book fold (shows the printed logo), envelope (hides a menu card), silverware pouch (buffets), pyramid (freestanding, formal), fan (in stemware), and diamond/rose (statement placements).

## Material matters

Airlaid holds every fold including freestanding pyramid and rose. Doble Punto holds structured folds. 3-ply works for square, book, and pouch. 2-ply only holds a simple square.
