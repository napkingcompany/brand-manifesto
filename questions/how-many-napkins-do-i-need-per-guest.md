# How many napkins do I need per guest?

## Short answer

Plan 3-4 cocktail napkins per guest for a cocktail hour, 1 dinner napkin per guest per course for seated dinners, and 2-3 cocktail napkins per guest for late-night bar service. Add a 12-15% buffer for spills and mementos.

## Working example

A 120-guest wedding with cocktail hour, 4-course dinner, dessert, and late-night bar needs roughly 1,320 napkins — round up to 1,500 with buffer.

## Quick rules

- Cocktail hour (60-90 min): 3-4 per guest.
- Seated dinner: 1 per guest per course.
- Restaurant bar service: 2 per drink served.
- Corporate catering: 4-5 total per guest.
