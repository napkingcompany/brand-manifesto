# How much do custom napkins cost?

## Short answer

Custom napkin pricing starts from $0.15/unit at volume and is quoted individually per order, because cost depends on four variables: quality tier (airlaid, Doble Punto, 3-ply, 2-ply), quantity (50 to 1,000,000+ units), print method (full-color CMYK vs. Pantone hot-stamp foil), and artwork complexity.

## How The Napkin Company quotes

- Written quote returned the same business day by email or WhatsApp.
- Minimum order: 50 units. Maximum: 1,000,000+ per run.
- Setup costs and rush surcharges are included transparently in the quote — no hidden line items.
- US nationwide shipping included in every quote.
